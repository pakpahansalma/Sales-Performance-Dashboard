# 🚗 Car Sales Performance Dashboard — Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Car Sales Performance Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance across different business dimensions.

The dashboard provides an overview of key sales metrics and allows users to explore sales performance by **brand, body style, color, dealer region, transmission, engine, and time period**.

The main objective of this project is to demonstrate how sales data can be transformed into **clear business insights and interactive performance reporting**.

> **Dataset:** Public dataset
> **Tool:** Microsoft Power BI
> **Project Type:** Sales Performance Analysis & Dashboarding

---

## 🎯 Business Questions

This dashboard was developed to answer several business-oriented questions:

* How is overall sales performance?
* How does sales performance change over time?
* Which brands contribute the most to total sales?
* How do sales volume and average price contribute to revenue?
* How does sales performance differ across product categories and regions?
* Which periods show significant changes in sales performance?

The analysis follows this workflow:

**Sales Data → KPI & Performance Analysis → Sales Contribution Analysis → Trend Analysis → Interactive Dashboard**

Analyzed key performance indicators including:

* Total Sales
* YTD Sales
* MTD Sales
* Sales Growth
* Cars Sold
* Average Price

### 2. Sales Contribution Analysis

Compared sales contribution across different brands and categories to identify major contributors to overall revenue.

### 3. Trend Analysis

Analyzed sales performance over time to identify periods with higher or lower sales activity and potential areas for further investigation.

### 4. Interactive Dashboard

* KPI Cards
* Line Charts
* Bar Charts
* Donut Charts

## 📈 Key Findings

Some key observations from the analysis include:

### Overall Sales Performance

* Total Sales: **$371.2M**
* Cars Sold: **13.3K**
* Transactions: **23.9K**
* YTD Sales Growth: **23.59%**
* YTD Cars Sold Growth: **19.73%**
* Average Price: approximately **$28.0K**

### Sales Contribution

**Chevrolet** was the largest sales contributor in the analyzed dataset, accounting for approximately **7.30% of total sales**, supported by relatively high unit volume.

The analysis also shows that brand performance should not be evaluated using revenue alone. **Sales volume and average price need to be considered together** to better understand the drivers of sales performance.

### Sales Trend

The weekly sales analysis showed a notable sales peak around **week 38**, which can be used as a point for further investigation into the factors behind the increase.

---

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis highlights that sales performance is influenced by multiple factors.

Looking only at revenue may not provide enough context. Combining **sales value, unit volume, average price, brand contribution, and time trends** provides a more complete view of sales performance.

The dashboard can therefore be used as a starting point to:

* Monitor sales performance
* Identify major sales contributors
* Compare brands and categories
* Investigate significant changes in sales
* Support data-driven sales discussions
